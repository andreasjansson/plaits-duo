How to

Install the firmware

  1. , unzip it.
  2. Patch a cable from your computer's or phone's headphone output into MODEL. FREQUENCY at noon.
  3. Power the module on holding the left button.
  4. Notifications off (any other sound spoils it), then play the WAV. The level meter rises from the 4th LED: set FREQUENCY for 2 or 3 lit. The bottom LEDs circle as it loads. All red: press left, play louder.

Switch to duo mode

  • Both buttons: duo mode. Again: the original firmware.
  • Left: stereo models (yellow). Right: split (red).

Calibrate Do this once

  • In duo mode, hold both buttons for 2 s: the bottom LED pulses green, and OUT buzzes (turn your mixer down).
  • Patch OUT into V/OCT, wait a second, press left: the LED turns yellow.
  • Move the cable to LEVEL, wait a second, press left: the model's LED is back. Done and saved.
  • All red: wrong jack, press a button and retry. Uncalibrated, voice 2 can be 25 cents per octave off.

Usage

  • V/OCT and LEVEL: the pitches of voice 1 and voice 2 (LEVEL stops at about −0.7V). Empty LEVEL: one voice. TRIGGER pings both.
  • Stereo: OUT left, AUX right. Split: voice 1 on OUT, voice 2 on AUX.
