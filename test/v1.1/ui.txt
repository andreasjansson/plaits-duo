===
the left button cycles through the green models, the right one through the red models
===
cat > script.txt <<'SCRIPT'
wait 500ms
leds
tap left
leds
tap left
leds
tap right
leds
tap right
leds
tap left
leds
SCRIPT
plaits-sim < script.txt > new.txt
plaits-sim-v1.1 < script.txt > reference.txt
diff new.txt reference.txt && cat new.txt
---
leds G.......
leds .G......
leds ..G.....
leds ..R.....
leds ...R....
leds ...G....

===
holding the left button shows the low-pass gate color and decay, and TIMBRE and MORPH change them
===
cat > script.txt <<'SCRIPT'
wait 500ms
press left
wait 2.1s
leds
knob timbre 0.9
knob morph 0.1
wait 200ms
leds
release left
wait 100ms
leds
SCRIPT
plaits-sim < script.txt > new.txt
plaits-sim-v1.1 < script.txt > reference.txt
diff new.txt reference.txt && cat new.txt
---
leds ......YY
leds YYYY...Y
leds G.......
===
holding the right button shows the octave range, and HARMONICS changes it
===
cat > script.txt <<'SCRIPT'
wait 500ms
press right
wait 2.1s
leds
knob harmonics 0.35
wait 200ms
leds
release right
wait 100ms
leds
SCRIPT
plaits-sim < script.txt > new.txt
plaits-sim-v1.1 < script.txt > reference.txt
diff new.txt reference.txt && cat new.txt
---
leds YYYYYYYY
leds .....Y..
leds G.......
===
holding both buttons enters the V/OCT calibration (1V, then 3V)
===
cat > script.txt <<'SCRIPT'
wait 500ms
press both
wait 2.1s
release both
leds 500ms
cv v_oct 1
wait 200ms
tap left
leds 500ms
cv v_oct 3
wait 200ms
tap left
leds
SCRIPT
plaits-sim < script.txt > new.txt
plaits-sim-v1.1 < script.txt > reference.txt
diff new.txt reference.txt && cat new.txt
---
leds G.......
leds Y.......
leds G.......
